# Gift Cards

## Buying a gift card

Digital gift cards are available in amounts from $10 to $500. The gift card is delivered by email within 1 hour of purchase.

## Using a gift card

Up to 2 gift cards can be applied to a single order. A gift card can be combined with one rewards voucher. Any unused balance stays on the card.

## Expiry and refunds

Brindlewick Audio gift cards never expire. Gift cards cannot be refunded, returned, or exchanged for cash.
