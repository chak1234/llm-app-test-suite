# Shipping

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99. Orders with a subtotal of $75 or more ship free with standard shipping.

## Express shipping

Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.99. Express shipping is never free, including on orders that qualify for free standard shipping.

## Order processing

Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day. All parcels are carried by NorthPost Courier.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of the order shipping. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after the label is created.
