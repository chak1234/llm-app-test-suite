# Returns and Refunds

## Return window

You can return any Brindlewick Audio product within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must include its original packaging and all accessories. Returns requested after the 30 day window are not accepted.

## Refund timing and fees

Once the warehouse receives your return, the refund is issued within 7 business days to the original payment method. Opened items have a 15% restocking fee deducted from the refund. Unopened items are refunded in full.

## Return shipping

A prepaid return label costs $6.95, and the amount is deducted from your refund. If the item arrived defective or damaged, the return label is free.

## Items that cannot be returned

Clearance items marked "Final Sale" and digital gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.
