# Accounts and Login

## Passwords

Account passwords must be at least 12 characters long. A password reset link is sent by email and expires after 60 minutes.

## Account lockout

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. The lock clears on its own; support agents cannot unlock an account early.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification sends a 6-digit code to your email address at each new login. The code is valid for 10 minutes.

## Deleting your account

You can request account deletion from the Privacy page of your account settings. Deletion is completed within 14 days, and unused reward points are lost when the account is deleted.
