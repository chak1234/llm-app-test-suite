# Payments

## Accepted payment methods

Brindlewick Audio accepts Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal. Cash on delivery and personal checks are not accepted. All prices are charged in US dollars.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged when the order ships, not when the order is placed. A temporary authorization hold may appear at checkout and drops off within 3 business days.

## Paying in installments

Orders of $120 or more can be paid with SplitFour, which divides the total into 4 interest-free payments taken every 2 weeks. Gift card purchases cannot be paid with SplitFour.
