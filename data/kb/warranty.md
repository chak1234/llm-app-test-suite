# Warranty

## Warranty periods

Brindlewick Audio headphones have a warranty period of 12 months from the delivery date. Speakers have a warranty period of 24 months. Cables, ear tips, and other accessories have a warranty period of 3 months.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects, such as a dead driver, a faulty charging port, or a battery that holds less than 70% of its original capacity. It does not cover water damage, accidental drops, or normal cosmetic wear.

## How to make a warranty claim

Submit a claim through the Warranty Desk form with your order number and a photo of the defect. Claims are reviewed within 5 business days. Approved claims receive a replacement unit, not a cash refund.
