# Wick Rewards Program

## Earning points

Wick Rewards members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on products. Shipping fees and taxes do not earn points. Members also receive 50 bonus points on their birthday.

## Redeeming points

Every 200 points can be redeemed for a $10 voucher. Vouchers can be used on any product but not on gift cards. Only one voucher can be used per order.

## Point expiry

Points expire 18 months after they are earned. Vouchers expire 90 days after they are issued.
