# Out-of-Warranty Repairs

## Repair fees

Products outside their warranty period can be repaired for a flat fee. A headphone repair costs $39 and a speaker repair costs $59. The fee includes parts, labor, and return shipping.

## Repair turnaround

Repairs are completed within 10 business days of the product arriving at the Brindlewick repair center in Dayton, Ohio.

## Repair guarantee

Every repair comes with a 6 month guarantee on the replaced parts. If a product cannot be repaired, the repair fee is refunded in full and you receive a 20% discount code toward a replacement.
