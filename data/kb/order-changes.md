# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order within 45 minutes of placing it, from the Orders page of your account. After 45 minutes the order is sent to the warehouse and can no longer be cancelled; you can return it once it arrives.

## Changing the shipping address

The shipping address can be changed until the order status shows "Packed". After that, the address is locked and cannot be changed by support.

## Changing items

Items cannot be added to or swapped in an existing order. Cancel the order and place a new one instead. A cancelled order is refunded within 2 business days.
