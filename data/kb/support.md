# Contacting Support

## Support hours

The Brindlewick Audio support team is available Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time. Support is closed on weekends and US public holidays.

## Contact channels

You can reach support by email at help@brindlewick.example or by phone at 1-800-555-0142. Live chat is available on the website during support hours.

## Response times

Emails are answered within 1 business day. The average wait for live chat is under 4 minutes. Phone calls are not recorded.
